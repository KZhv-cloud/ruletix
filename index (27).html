    How it works

    1. 1
      Choose your dice

      1 to 10 dice with 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 or 20 sides.

    2. 2
      Roll

      Press the button or the space bar.

    3. 3
      Check

      See the total and your last 10 rolls.

    Ways to use it

    • When the board game dice go missing
    • Tabletop RPG sessions with a d20
    • Travel board games
    • Probability lessons in math class
    • Party games and dares
    • Choosing between 6 options

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the dice fair?

    Yes. Every side has the same chance thanks to the browser's cryptographic random generator.

    Does it work for RPGs?

    Yes: it includes d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20.

    Does it work offline?

    Once the page has loaded, yes: everything runs on your device.
