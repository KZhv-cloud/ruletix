    How it works

    1. 1
      Type your options

      One per line: names, meals, chores or anything you want to draw.

    2. 2
      Spin the wheel

      Press Spin, tap the center of the wheel or hit the space bar.

    3. 3
      See the winner

      Optionally remove the winner from the list for the next round.

    Ways to use it

    • Pick a student to answer in class
    • Decide what to eat or which movie to watch
    • Run a giveaway for followers or customers
    • Split household chores fairly
    • Choose who goes first in a board game
    • Pick random questions for a quiz

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the wheel really random?

    Yes. Each spin uses your browser's cryptographic random generator, so no result is more likely than another.

    How many options can I add?

    Up to 200. With many options the text gets smaller, but the draw works the same.

    Is my list saved?

    Your list is stored in the page link itself. Copy it with the Share button and it'll be there next time.

    Can I use it on a smart board?

    Yes. Use full screen mode and spin with the space bar or by tapping the center.
