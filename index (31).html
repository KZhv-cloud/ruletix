Cookie policy

What cookies are

Small files a website stores in your browser to remember information.

What we use

This site sets no cookies of its own. It stores a few preferences in your browser (local storage), like checked tasks or your saved list, which never leave your device. When ads are active, Google AdSense and its partners may set advertising and measurement cookies after you consent where required.

How to manage them

Change your consent from the privacy notice, or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.
