Paste your entrants, choose how many winners and alternates you need, and hit Draw. The result includes the date and time as proof.

Frequently asked questions

How do I run an Instagram giveaway?

Copy the list of eligible users (for example, everyone who commented), paste it here, remove duplicates and draw. Record your screen as proof.

What are alternates for?

If a winner doesn't reply or isn't eligible, the prize goes to the first alternate without redrawing.

Can the same person win twice?

No, unless they're listed twice. Turn on “Remove duplicates” so each name counts once.
